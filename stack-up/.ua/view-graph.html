Why Store Owners Love This Architecture:

Other discount apps store all data in one place, which causes checkout to crash when data exceeds Shopify's 10 KB limit. Our app uses Product-Level Sharding (only ~270 bytes per product), meaning merchants can run hundreds of discounts simultaneously with zero crashes and sub-second checkout speed.

📂 2. What Each File Does
Step 1 🖥️

Store Owner Sets Up the Discount

The merchant opens the admin panel and creates an offer like "Buy 2, Get 10% Off" or "Buy 1, Get 1 Free".

app/routes/app.offers.*.create.jsx
Step 2 🛡️

Safety Check (No Conflicts)

The app checks if the selected product already has another discount running, so two offers don't clash.

app/utils/productConflicts.server.js
Step 3 📦

Shrink Data to Avoid Crashes (~270 Bytes)

Shopify checkout crashes if discount data is bigger than 10 KB. Our app compresses the offer into a tiny ~270 bytes per product.

app/utils/*Metafields.server.js
Step 4 🛍️

Customer Sees the Offer Box

When a buyer opens the product page, our widget reads that tiny data and shows the discount box. Clicking "Add to Cart" puts items in the cart.

extensions/stack-up-theme-extension/
Step 5 ⚡

Auto Discount at Checkout

When the buyer clicks Checkout, Shopify instantly calculates the savings and cuts the price before they pay.

extensions/*-discount/
Step 6 📊

Track Orders & Revenue

When the order is placed, Shopify notifies our app. The app logs how much money this discount made and shows it on your dashboard.

app/routes/webhooks.orders.create.jsx
